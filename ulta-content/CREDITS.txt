ULTA AO2 - credits for bundled content
======================================

ULTA logo and title art
-----------------------
The ULTA logo and title art in the lobby was created by Marilith Cribaum
(Discord: marilithcribaum01).

Effects pack
------------
"Zutra's Vanilla Effects Pack v2 (CC Edition)"
Made by Zutra  -  Discord: _threedott
Bundled with ULTA AO2 with the author's permission. It provides the built-in
effects (the effects list, images, icons and a few sounds) found in the
"ulta/base" folder. Content in a player's own base folder always takes
priority over these built-in files.

Narrator content
----------------
The narrator content (the NarratorULTA characters, the DGSNarrator chat style
and the narrator voices/sounds) was compiled by Michael Notback (Discord: michael_notback).
Used with his permission.

Server and base content
-----------------------
The ULTA server (tsuserverULTA) is based on tsuserverCC by RealKaiser and the
Case Cafe staff (https://github.com/RealKaiser/tsuserverCC), which in turn is
a fork of tsuserver3 by the Attorney Online developers. Licensed under the
GNU Affero General Public License v3.
ULTA AO is designed to be used with a Case Cafe base install, which
players bring themselves. That content was made by jooj11 and guyfugs.

Libraries
---------
Discord Rich Presence uses the discord-rpc library by Discord, Inc. (MIT license,
included as ULTA-LICENSES/discord-rpc-LICENSE.txt).
Secure connections on Windows use OpenSSL 1.1.1 (OpenSSL license, included as
ULTA-LICENSES/openssl-LICENSE.txt).
Audio uses the BASS library by Un4seen Developments, free for non-commercial use.
